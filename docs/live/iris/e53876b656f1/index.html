Field guide

How to play

Each chamber has emitters and receivers. A beam keeps its color until it crosses a dye cell, which adds that cell's primary color. A mirror bends the beam a quarter turn.

  • Rotate a mirror with a click, tap, Enter or Space.
  • Move between mirrors with the arrow keys.
  • Charge a receiver only with its exact target hue.
  • Finish at or under par for three stars, within two extra moves for two.

Progress, stars and sound preferences are stored only in this browser.
